Odd Pairs Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Odd Pairs is a card matching game for Root, made by OddBitty. This page explains what information the game keeps, why it keeps it, and who can see it.

What the game keeps

To save your progress and run the community features, the game keeps:

  • Your game progress, saved with your Root user ID so it follows you to any device in the same community. This includes:
    • the specimens you have found, how many of each you have, and their levels
    • your ship design and the ship parts you have earned or are still to pick
    • your achievements (with when you earned each one) and the titles they gave you
    • your game totals, such as wins, losses, best score, best combo and win streak, and small counters the game uses, such as how many times Odd has swapped your cards
    • whether you have won today's Daily Odd
    • the game you are playing right now, including any power up you brought and any spares you risked, so that leaving part way through counts as a loss
    • which tips you have seen, and your chosen deck and power up
    • whether you have switched on the ship gallery, and how many boosts your ship has had
  • Found counts: for each specimen, the number of members in the community who have found it, and the number of members who have found at least one. These are counts only. To count each member once, the game also keeps a list of which specimens it has already counted for your Root user ID.
  • The Daily Odd board: when you win the Daily Odd, your Root user ID, your Root nickname and your best score that day go on that day's board. Up to 50 entries are kept for each day, and each day's board is removed after about a week.
  • The ship gallery, only if you switch on "Show my ship in the gallery" on your ship screen. The game then keeps:
    • your ship design, your Root nickname and your title, saved with your Root user ID and a random ship ID
    • the date and time you first shared your ship, used to sort the Newest tab
    • how many boosts your ship has had in total, and how many this week (this count starts again every Monday, UTC)
    • a list of the Root user IDs of members who boosted your ship, so each member can only boost it once
    When you boost someone else's ship, your Root user ID goes on that ship's list. If you switch the gallery off, your ship is hidden from everyone, but the game keeps your entry and boosts so they are still there if you switch it back on. Gallery entries are not removed on their own. To have yours removed, email us at the address below.

The player count

While the game is open on your screen, it tells the server you are still playing every few seconds. The server uses this to show how many people are playing in the game's channel description, for example "2 players taking on Odd". Only the number is shown. The server holds this in memory for about 15 seconds and does not save it.

The game's server log notes how many people are playing and any problems it runs into. If the player count cannot be updated, a note about the problem also goes to the community's app log, which members who can manage apps can see. None of these notes say who you are.

What the game does not keep

  • The game does not save your profile picture or any other details from your Root profile, apart from your nickname on the Daily Odd board and in the ship gallery.
  • The game does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • The game does not send your information to any service outside Root.

Where your information is stored

Your progress, the found counts, the Daily Odd boards and the ship gallery are stored in the storage that Root provides for the game. Each community where the game is installed has its own copy, so your progress in one community is separate from another. The first time you play in a community, you start fresh. Root's own privacy policy covers how Root stores and protects that data.

Your device also keeps a copy of your progress, so the game can still start if the server is slow to answer. This copy is never sent to the server in place of your saved progress. If the server cannot be reached when you open the game, you can choose to play anyway, and anything you win in that session stays on your device only. Your device also remembers whether the sound is on, which sound style you picked and, until you close the game, whether animations were switched off because the device was running slowly. These stay on your device.

Who can see it

  • Members of the community can see the top 10 on each day's Daily Odd board, with each player's nickname and score. The game does not send other players' Root user IDs to your device.
  • If you are on the board but not in the top 10, you can see your own place.
  • Members can see how many people in the community have found each specimen. They cannot see who.
  • If you switch on the ship gallery, members of the community can see your ship, your nickname, your title and how many boosts it has had. If your ship is the most boosted this week, they also see how many boosts it had this week. They cannot see who boosted it, and you cannot see who boosted yours. The game sends the random ship ID to their devices, not your Root user ID. It also sends each ship's boosts this week and the date it was first shared, which are used to sort the gallery.
  • Apart from a ship you share in the gallery, your game progress is only shown to you.
